SubLimit — Services demo: payroll, subcontracting limit and monthly workforce report
Narrated walkthrough with a fictional facilities maintenance contractor (order FAC-DEMO-002, October 2026)

00:00 — A fictional services contractor
This demo uses fictional data: an invented facilities maintenance contractor and its records. On the Overview page, choose Open configuration and load the practice contract file.

00:12 — Contract setup
Contract setup shows order FAC-DEMO-002 for October 2026: a services contract under NAICS 561210.

00:23 — Facts with evidence
Each answer, such as whether FAR 52.219-14 applies, carries an evidence reference. Here they are marked synthetic.

00:33 — Compliance period
The compliance period is October 1 to 31, 2026, with a 5-point warning margin.

00:41 — Organize people
People & vendors gives every employee and vendor a stable ID. Seven fictional employees are listed.

00:48 — Organize subcontractors
Four subcontractors follow. Verification shows one verified similarly situated and three verified not similarly situated.

00:57 — A similarly situated subcontractor
Sample Janitorial Partners shows the facts behind its status: verified similarly situated, valid through 2026, first tier, and small under NAICS 561210.

01:10 — Government receipts
Government receipts holds the payment basis: $72,400 and $68,150, a total of $140,550.

01:22 — Import the payroll export
In Import & mapping, select payroll.xlsx under Payroll / timekeeping. The saved mapping matches each required field to its column heading.

01:33 — Preview the payroll rows
Preview first file shows the source rows. Line P108 belongs to another job, FAC-OTHER-009.

01:42 — Import subcontractor payments
Then select subcontractors.xlsx, mapped for amount paid, further subcontracted amounts, hours and materials.

01:52 — Subcontractor payments
Besides the October payments, it includes a payment on the other job and one dated November 3.

01:58 — Import and calculate
Choose Import and calculate. Processing stays on this computer.

02:04 — Monitor the limit
Results shows COMPLIANT for the final October period, against a 50% limit.

02:10 — Percentage against the limit
Counted subcontracting is $59,150 against $140,550 in eligible Government payments: 42.08%, about eight points under the limit.

02:26 — Monthly workforce totals
Workforce totals: 7 prime employees with 928 work hours, and 650 subcontractor work hours.

02:35 — What was counted
Each counted record is listed. Payments to the three subcontractors that are not similarly situated count in full.

02:43 — The similarly situated subcontractor
For the similarly situated subcontractor, record S102, only the $3,500 it further subcontracted counts.

02:53 — Trace to the source row
Double-click a row to trace it to the worksheet, row and original values: a $21,000 payment, with $3,500 further subcontracted.

03:04 — Rows kept, not counted
View all source rows shows the other-job payment excluded as other contract, and the November payment excluded as outside the period.

03:12 — Exceptions
Exceptions is empty for this sample. Check it on every run.

03:18 — Monthly workforce report
For the monthly workforce report, open OCC workforce report and choose Import staffing file to load staffing.json.

03:27 — Workers and hours
13 worker lines load, with all imported hours reconciled.

03:32 — Subcontractor workers
Subcontractor workers are listed with their employer and hours. In total, 1,578 hours: 928 prime and 650 subcontractor.

03:44 — Contractor facts
Contractor facts holds the fictional UEI, company name and contract number, plus the form confirmations.

03:52 — Export the unsigned draft
Export unsigned Word draft saves the draft locally, and the status line confirms it was verified.

03:59 — The draft document
The draft says Unsigned draft, review required, and the signature line is blank. Your authorized officer reviews, signs and submits it. SubLimit does not file anything.

04:12 — Recap
Organize the records, monitor the limit, prepare the workforce draft. SubLimit by ProfitPin Group is in the Microsoft Store with a 30-day trial.
