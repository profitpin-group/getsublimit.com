How to download SubLimit for Windows

  1. Recommended: get SubLimit from the Microsoft Store. Microsoft signs the Store version, so Windows shows no unknown-publisher warning.
  2. Or download the installer (version 0.7.13, 41.3 MB, no payment to download). It is currently unsigned. If Windows blocks it, contact support rather than bypassing the warning.
  3. Close any older SubLimit window, then run SubLimit-0.7.13-Setup.exe from your Downloads list and complete setup.
  4. Open SubLimit. The first time, read the one-time agreement, tick the box and choose I agree.

How to set up a contract for the limitations on subcontracting

  1. Choose Overview → New contract, then open Contract setup.
  2. Enter the Contract / order identifier, Reporting month (YYYY-MM), Contract category, Contract NAICS and Prime program status.
  3. Answer “Does FAR 52.219-14 apply to this contract?” and add the applicability evidence reference. If you are not sure, leave it blank and ask your contracting officer or compliance adviser.
  4. Answer the prime status, joint venture, nonmanufacturer and mixed contract questions. Unknown answers stay blank.
  5. Enter the compliance period start, end and basis (base, option or order) with its evidence reference. The warning margin starts at 5.00 percentage points.
  6. Choose Save local draft.

How to enter employees, subcontractors and government payments

  1. Open People & vendors and choose Add entity.
  2. Enter a Persistent entity ID, the Entity name and the Type: employee or subcontractor. Independent contractors belong under subcontractors.
  3. Enter Active from. Leave Active through blank if the person or vendor is still active.
  4. Under Exact identity mappings, add one line per source: source system | external key. Use the same source system name you enter under Import & mapping, and the key that appears in the file’s entity key column. Choose Apply to draft.
  5. Open Government receipts and choose Add receipt. Enter the receipt ID, contract, date, Government amount paid (USD), included material cost (an explicit 0 if none) and the evidence reference.
  6. Enter receipts for the full compliance window. Payroll cost is not the Government-payment denominator.

How to track a similarly situated subcontractor

  1. In People & vendors, select a subcontractor and choose Edit selected.
  2. Set Subcontractor verification status: UNVERIFIED, VERIFIED_SSE (similarly situated) or VERIFIED_NON_SSE.
  3. For either verified status, enter Verification valid from, Verification valid through, the evidence reference and Verified by. A payment only counts as verified when its date falls inside those dates.
  4. For VERIFIED_SSE, also enter the Assigned subcontract NAICS, Subcontractor program status and Size evidence reference, and answer Yes to First-tier subcontractor and Small under assigned NAICS only when your evidence shows it.
  5. For a verified similarly situated subcontractor, only the amount it further subcontracted counts. For a verified non-SSE subcontractor, the payment less materials counts. Unverified payments are flagged and block the result.

How to import payroll and subcontractor spreadsheets

  1. Open Import & mapping. Under Payroll / timekeeping and under Subcontractor payments / time, choose Select CSV / XLSX files. Both are required; if you had no subcontractors, use a header-only export.
  2. Fill in the stable source system name, the exact worksheet name (XLSX), the header row number, the date pattern and the CSV delimiter.
  3. Map each required field to its exact column heading. Payroll: Record id, Entity key, Contract id, Date, Hours, Labor cost. Subcontractors: the same first five plus Work date, Amount paid, Materials and Further subcontracted.
  4. Choose Preview first file to check the headings.
  5. Under the coverage boxes, enter the dates each source is complete from and through, confirm complete coverage, and add the reconciliation evidence. Then choose Save draft.
  6. Each file can be up to 30 MB. Cells with formulas need a values-only export. Rows for another contract are excluded.

How to calculate the subcontracting limit

  1. On Import & mapping, choose Import and calculate. New calculations need an active trial or subscription.
  2. Results shows one status: COMPLIANT, APPROACHING LIMIT, NONCOMPLIANT or REVIEW REQUIRED. REVIEW REQUIRED means a blocking item exists or the compliance window has not reached its end yet.
  3. Read the three figures: Counted subcontracting (percent), Counted amount and Eligible Government payments. The percent is the counted amount divided by Government payments less excluded materials.
  4. The limit is 50% for services and supplies, 85% for general construction and 75% for special trade construction. APPROACHING LIMIT means you are within the warning margin.
  5. Double-click a payment contribution to see its source rows. Review Exceptions even when the result is compliant.
  6. Save the result with Export package, Print / save as PDF or Export CSV. Every run stays in History.

How to prepare the monthly workforce report

  1. Calculate a report, or open one from History with Open selected run.
  2. Open OCC workforce report and choose New staffing (or Import staffing file, or Load saved draft).
  3. Under Contractor facts, confirm that your contract requires the OCC form dated August 6, 2026, and enter the evidence reference.
  4. Choose Add worker. Enter the worker facts. Under Source records and hours, pick an imported work record, enter the hours for this worker and choose Add allocation. Then choose Apply to staffing draft.
  5. Clear Items needing attention, then choose Save staffing draft.
  6. Choose Export unsigned Word draft. It is available only when facts and hours reconcile. Check every page. Your officer reviews and signs; SubLimit does not sign or submit.

How to fix a flagged item in SubLimit

  1. Open Exceptions. Each row shows the State, Problem, Field and Source row.
  2. Select a row and choose Open selected detail to see the file, sheet and row.
  3. Fix the cause: choose Edit contract facts or Edit identity mappings, or correct the source export. Do not guess a missing fact.
  4. Choose Import and calculate again. The earlier run stays in History.
  5. For a workforce draft item, open OCC workforce report → Items needing attention, select it and choose Correct selected item.

How to back up SubLimit and move to a second computer

  1. Open Backup & restore and choose Create local backup. Pick where to save the file.
  2. Choose a passphrase of at least 12 characters and enter it again. Keep it safely; it cannot be recovered.
  3. On the other computer, open Backup & restore, choose Restore into a new folder, select the backup and enter its passphrase. SubLimit always restores into a new folder.
  4. Open History and check your reports before relying on the backup.
  5. To activate the second computer, open Overview → Activation, sign in with the same email and choose Restore access — already subscribed. One subscription covers two Windows computers. Files do not sync between them, and a backup does not move activation.

Common questions about the limitations on subcontracting

How much can I subcontract on a federal contract?

It depends on your contract and its clauses. Read FAR 52.219-14 and your contract, and ask your contracting officer if you are unsure. When you confirm the clause applies, SubLimit uses these limits by contract category: 50% for services and supplies, 85% for general construction and 75% for special trade construction, measured against eligible Government payments.

Does SubLimit check FAR 52.219-14 compliance?

SubLimit helps you track it. It calculates from the records you import and the facts you confirm, and it flags what is missing. It does not certify compliance or give legal advice. Your authorized officer reviews the result. Read the clause itself at acquisition.gov/far/52.219-14.

What are the subcontracting rules for a set-aside contract?

SubLimit does not decide which rules apply to your set-aside contract. You answer “Does FAR 52.219-14 apply to this contract?” in Contract setup and record your evidence. If you cannot confirm it, leave the answer blank and ask your contracting officer or compliance adviser.

Does SubLimit handle the 8(a) subcontracting limit?

Choose 8a as the Prime program status in Contract setup. The calculation uses the same category limits listed above. A subcontractor counts as similarly situated in SubLimit only when its program status matches yours, unless your status is small_business. Confirm the rules for your contract in FAR 52.219-14.
