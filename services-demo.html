What the demo covers

  • Records: contract setup with evidence for each answer, employees and subcontractors with stable IDs and similarly situated status, Government receipts, and payroll and subcontractor spreadsheets imported through saved column mappings.
  • The limit: fictional order FAC-DEMO-002 for October 2026 counts $59,150.00 of $140,550.00 in eligible Government payments, 42.084667% against the 50% services limit, with every counted row traceable to its source. Rows from another job or another month are kept but not counted.
  • The monthly workforce report: a staffing file imported into 13 worker lines (1,578 hours), checked, and exported as an unsigned Word draft for the authorized officer to review.

SubLimit prepares calculations and drafts for your review; it does not file anything or give legal advice. Your records stay on your own computer. SubLimit is in the Microsoft Store with a 30-day trial and no card. Questions: support@getsublimit.com.
